01 · About

Background

I'm a cybersecurity researcher with a focus on AI/ML security — how machine learning models behave (and fail) when applied to real endpoint telemetry, and how adversaries exploit hardware and firmware at the signal level. My work spans ransomware detection pipelines built on Sysmon data, side-channel and fault-injection attacks on embedded systems, and building tools that make cybersecurity education more rigorous.

I'm a first-author on an IEEE SoutheastCon 2026 publication and have two additional papers under review. I graduate from the University of North Georgia in May 2026 with a B.S. in Cybersecurity, and begin my M.S. in Cybersecurity at Georgia Institute of Technology in August 2026.

Alongside research, I've worked in cybersecurity examination and operational resilience at the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta, and run my own web development and consulting practice.
